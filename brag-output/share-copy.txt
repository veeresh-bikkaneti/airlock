One line installs Airlock. It detects your hardware, picks Ollama or vLLM, measures your real RAM/VRAM and pulls the model that actually fits — no guessing — then proves the whole setup with a real test chat before you ever touch it. No cloud rate limits, no flaky routing layer. Proof, not a claim. Switch from Claude Code to Grok, Pi, or OpenCode mid-task and pick up right where you left off — no re-explaining.
